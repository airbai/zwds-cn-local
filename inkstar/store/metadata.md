## Notes for App Review

Inkstar presents a traditional Chinese star-chart system for self-reflection and cultural interest. Readings are generated by a language model constrained to the chart computed on device; the app does not predict events.

No account or login is needed. To try it: enter any name, birth date, time window and gender. The chart, the Today tab and three questions a day are free. The paywall opens from Me → See Inkstar Plus, or after the free questions are used.

In-app purchases: inkstar_plus_yearly (7-day trial), inkstar_plus_monthly, inkstar_plus_lifetime.

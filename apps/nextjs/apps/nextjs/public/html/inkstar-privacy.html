Privacy Policy

Inkstar · Effective 16 November 2026

Inkstar is an app that draws a traditional Chinese twelve-palace star chart from your birth details. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

What stays on your device

  • Your name, birth date, birth time window and gender.
  • Your chart, your language choice and your conversation history.

The app has no account system. Erasing your data in the app (Me → Erase my data) or deleting the app removes all of it.

What is sent to our server

When you ask a question, the app sends the following to our server so that an answer can be written:

  • the text of your question and the recent messages of that conversation;
  • the chart computed from your birth details (birth date, time window, gender, and star positions) — your name is not sent;
  • your language, and a random identifier created by the app, used only to limit abuse.

Our server passes the question and chart to a language-model provider to generate the answer. We do not store your questions or answers on our server. We keep a daily count of requests per random identifier, which expires within about a day.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is active; it receives the random identifier and purchase records from Apple, not your name or birth details. We never see your payment card.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Children

Inkstar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can erase everything the app holds at any time from the Me tab. For any question or request about your data, write to dothingsatonce@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
